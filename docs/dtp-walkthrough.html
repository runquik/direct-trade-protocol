Built to sit under your tools.

DTP deliberately stops at the property line. Everything private stays in the tools a team already uses. Everything between counterparties runs on the protocol.

Your toolkit's side · private

  • Cost targets, budgets, and margin floors
  • Inventory and reorder points
  • Supplier preferences and history
  • Escalate-to-human thresholds

Held off-chain in the agent's autonomy context. Never visible to a counterparty.

The protocol's side · shared

  • Portable identity: KYB, GS1 GLN, D-U-N-S, certifications
  • Intents, listings, offers, contracts
  • Escrow and USDC settlement rules
  • FSMA 204 traceability and earned reputation

Open objects any conforming agent can read, post, and verify.

Agents on both sides speak DTP the way apps speak HTTP. Any AI toolkit for CPG teams can be the interface. The protocol is the road.
