# `dtp/product@1`

Status: **registered** in [`spec/profiles/index.json`](../index.json) on September 23, 2026, after a second, separately coded implementation (`sdk/tests/interop/product-reader.ts`) passed the fixtures. A host admits the exact contract in [`1/profile.json`](1/profile.json) (publisher `dtp`, semantics `product-v1`) with `profile.admit`; the registry pins its digest.

## Name and version

Kind `dtp/product@1`; profile `dtp.product/1`; contract version `1.0.0`. Minor versions within the major MUST be additive.

## Nature

A **state** kind. One record chain is one product as one company defines it. The head is the current statement; revisions supersede.

## Identity

- The entity is the record `root_id`, the writer's id at genesis. There is no natural business key: a GTIN or a SKU is an identifier the company records, namespaced by its scheme, not the identity.
- A revision is the record `record_id`. Facts that cite a product as evidence (an order line, a stock move) name the exact revision; facts that merely refer to the product name the root.
- Every `dtp/inventory@2` ledger is keyed by this root, and its rules depend on `base_unit` and `tracking`; that is why those are immutable (below).

## Payload

The schema is the bounded `dtp.schema/1` dialect with `nullable`, in [`1/profile.json`](1/profile.json). In prose:

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `names` | 1 to 8 `{name, language}`; the first is the primary name; `language` is a BCP 47-style tag or null. |
| `description`, `brand`, `category` | Bounded text or null. `category` is the company's own word; no taxonomy is imposed. |
| `identifiers` | 0 to 32 `{scheme, value}`, distinct. `scheme` is a lowercase dotted name. `gs1.gtin` is check-digit validated (GTIN-8, -12, -13, -14). `sku` is the company's own and appears at most once. Every other scheme is opaque and namespaced by the writer's choice; mapping two identifiers is a claim by the writer, not proof they name one thing. |
| `base_unit` | `{system: "ucum", code}`. The unit every stock quantity of this product is expressed in. Syntax only: the profile does not convert units, ever. Counted units use UCUM annotations, for example `{jar}` or `{unit}`. |
| `packaging` | 0 to 32 revisions `{packaging_id, version, name, base_units_per_pack, gtin}`, distinct by id and version. `base_units_per_pack` is a positive three-place decimal string. A packaging revision is what a stock event pins when it counts in packs. |
| `tracking` | `none`, `lot` or `serial`: whether stock positions of this product carry a lot id, and whether moves list serials. |
| `shelf_life_days` | Positive integer or null. |
| `replaces` | Root id of the product this one replaces, or null. |
| `status` | `active` or `discontinued`. |

Decimals are strings; there are no JSON floats. Member names follow the repository rule.

## Semantics (`product-v1`)

A host that admits a profile with these semantics runs the rules below on every write, whatever the publisher's schema says; a publisher selects the label and cannot loosen the rules.

1. The body passes every rule in the payload table (`validateProduct`).
2. Across a supersession of one root (`checkProductContinuity`): `base_unit`, `tracking` and `replaces` are equal in the previous and the next revision; every packaging `(packaging_id, version)` in the previous revision is present in the next with the same `name`, `base_units_per_pack` and `gtin`. New packaging revisions may be added; nothing is removed or altered. A change to any of these is a **new product** whose `replaces` names the old root.
3. A refused write is `invalid_product`; snapshot import re-checks both rules.

Rule 2 exists because a stock event that counted in packs pinned a packaging revision, and a ledger keyed on a base unit cannot survive that unit changing under it.

## Relations

- `dtp/inventory@2` keys ledgers by this root and expresses quantities in `base_unit` or through a packaging revision listed here; its unit rules are this profile's.
- The v0.2 `goods_spec` inside a trade record MAY cite a product by root; the reverse is never true.
- `dtp.packaging/1` (the v1 inventory packaging revision) is superseded by inline `packaging` here for products of this kind; v1 pools keep their own.

## Not in scope

Variants, bills of materials, images, pricing, a category taxonomy, unit conversion, and any claim that a GTIN is licensed to the writer. A product record is a company's statement about its own product, not a registration.
