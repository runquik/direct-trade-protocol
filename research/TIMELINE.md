# Timeline: Physical-Goods Trade Coordination, 1930–2026

One merged chronology across all eight dossiers. **Bold** = load-bearing events for DTP's design. Dossier references in parentheses.

## Pre-history: the legal rails

- **1930** — Perishable Agricultural Commodities Act (PACA); amended **1984** to create the statutory floating trust giving unpaid produce sellers super-priority over all creditors. **The reason DTP's produce terms cap at 30 days — beyond that the trust is forfeited.** (08)
- **1936** — ICC publishes the first Incoterms. Semantics-only standardization; now used in 140+ countries. (08)
- **1948** — Berlin Airlift: Ed Guilbert's standardized radio-teletype shipping manifests — EDI's conceptual origin. (01)

## The EDI era (1965–2000)

- **1965** — Holland-America line sends trans-Atlantic shipping manifests by telex. (01)
- **1968** — Transportation Data Coordinating Committee (TDCC) formed; first true EDI standards published **1975**. (01)
- **1969–1973** — Grocery industry Ad Hoc Committee (manufacturers + retailers jointly) selects the UPC barcode (Apr 3, 1973); Uniform Code Council founded **1974**; **first UPC scan June 26, 1974** (Wrigley's gum, Troy, Ohio). Adversaries co-owning a neutral standard + a physical artifact = the most successful B2B standard ever. (03)
- **1979** — ANSI charters ASC X12. (01)
- **~1980–82** — UCS: grocery's EDI subset; identity (UPC) and messaging (UCS) governed under one roof. (01)
- **1987** — UN/EDIFACT syntax approved as ISO 9735. X12/EDIFACT never merge — installed base wins over harmonization. (01)
- **1990s** — Quick Response/JIT retail: EDI becomes a condition of shelf space. Adoption research finds coercive customer pressure, not ROI, drives small-supplier adoption. (01)

## The XML wave (1995–2010)

- **1995** — OAGIS BODs (ERP vendors). FreeMarkets founded (reverse auctions). (02, 04)
- **1997** — OBI v1.0 (Fortune 500 buyers). Chemdex founded. (02, 04)
- **1998** — RosettaNet founded (~40 IT firms); XML 1.0 W3C recommendation; e-STEEL, MetalSite, PaperExchange launch. (02, 04)
- **1999** — cXML published by Ariba; ebXML launched (UN/CEFACT + OASIS, 18-month grand design); **iTradeNetwork founded** (the food-network survivor); escrow.com founded; Alibaba founded; Chemdex IPOs on $165K quarterly sales. (02, 04, 08)
- **Feb–Apr 2000** — Peak mania: Ventro (Chemdex) ~$10–11B market cap; Covisint ($500M, Big Three); Transora (~$250M, 50 CPG firms); GNX vs WWRE; Gartner's $7.3T B2B forecast lands three weeks after the NASDAQ peak. (04)
- **Dec 2000** — Ventro shuts Chemdex. **2001**: mass extinction — MetalSite, efdex ($69M burned), food exchanges, Rooster.com; e-STEEL exits. **1,520 exchanges (2001) → ~180 (2003).** (04)
- **2001–2004** — ebXML fragments (only ebMS messaging survives); UBL chartered at OASIS, v1.0 ratified **Nov 2004**. (02)
- **Sep 9, 2002** — **Walmart mandates AS2**: a hub unilaterally re-platforms the ecosystem's transport layer; VAN economics collapse. The canonical hub-led transition. (01)
- **2002** — RosettaNet folds into GS1 US umbrella; GS1 US shifts prefixes to annual renewal fees (lawsuits, gray market, durable resentment). (02, 03)
- **Jun 2003** — **Walmart's RFID mandate** (top 100 suppliers): suppliers pay, Walmart benefits — dead by 2007. The defining asymmetric-cost failure. (03)
- **2004–2005** — Covisint sold for parts ($293M vs $500M in); FreeMarkets absorbed by Ariba; Transora+UCCnet → 1SYNC (the food consortium survives only as GS1 data-sync); GNX+WWRE → Agentrics; **Denmark mandates UBL for public invoices** — the first e-invoicing forcing function. (02, 04)
- **Jan 2006** — IBM/Microsoft/SAP shut the UDDI public registry: **public B2B registries rot without a curator.** (02)
- **2007** — EPCIS 1.0 ratified. **2008** — Peppol project starts; PTI launched (voluntary produce traceability — drifts for five years). (02, 03)

## Consolidation and quiet compounding (2010–2016)

- **2010** — iTradeNetwork sold to Roper for $525M — the surviving food network is procurement plumbing on existing relationships, >50% EBITDA margins. (08)
- **2012** — SAP buys Ariba for $4.3B; the "open format, closed network" tollbooth at $319B/yr flow. (02, 04)
- **Nov 2013** — **Walmart makes PTI labels a condition of purchase** — voluntary traceability finally moves. (03)
- **2014** — EU Directive 2014/55 (e-invoicing law behind UBL/Peppol's hockey stick); OpenBazaar forked from DarkMarket; ProducePay and GrubMarket founded; **Alibaba launches Trade Assurance** — platform escrow arrives *after* liquidity exists, then covers 160M+ orders. (02, 06, 08)
- **2015** — Everledger founded; Hyperledger Fabric; Faire founded **2017** with the net-60/instant-pay wedge. (05, 08)

## The blockchain consortium wave (2016–2023)

- **Dec 2016** — **AgriDigital/CBH pilot: world's first physical-commodity settlement on blockchain (grain title vs payment)** — the one settlement-first experiment; everyone else anchors data. DTP's true ancestor. (05)
- **2017–2018** — TradeLens (Maersk+IBM), IBM Food Trust, we.trade (12 banks), Marco Polo, komgo, B3i, Voltron→Contour; VeChain, OriginTrail token launches; Indigo Marketplace launches on a $250M Series E. (05, 08)
- **Sep 2018** — Walmart mandates Food Trust for leafy-greens suppliers (compliance-driven, not demand-driven). (05)
- **2019** — Gartner predicts 90% blockchain-supply-chain fatigue by 2023 — accurate. Beckn Protocol created (Nilekani/Varma/Nair). (05, 06)
- **2020–2021** — OpenBazaar shuts (Jan 2021: ~6,651 lifetime users on $9.25M VC); Boson raises ~$36M, spends $709K on Decentraland land; Indigo removes itself from the transaction flow (Dec 2021) — marketplace → software for the elevators it tried to displace; IBM blockchain unit gutted after ~90% revenue misses. (05, 06, 08)
- **Nov 2022** — FSMA 204 final rule (compliance date Jan 20, 2026); EPCIS 2.0 ratified (Jun); Walmart's RFID revival works this time (tags ~3–5¢, suppliers capture value too); Boson v2 live; ONDC live pilot (Apr); Namma Yatri launches (Nov). **TradeLens announces shutdown** despite ~half of global container data connected. (03, 05, 06)
- **2022–2023** — **The consortium die-off**: we.trade insolvent (Jun 2022), B3i insolvent (Jul 2022), Marco Polo insolvent (Feb 2023, €5.2M debts), Everledger administration (Apr 2023), Contour wound down (Nov 2023, 60–70 tx/month). komgo survives by dropping blockchain. FarmLead marketplace already sunset (2020) → Bayer (2023); ChefHero→Notch fintech pivot; Cheetah sells its trucks (2024). (05, 08)

## The agent wave (2024–2026)

- **Nov 2024** — Anthropic releases MCP; becomes the agent tool-access substrate. (07)
- **Apr–May 2025** — Google A2A; Mastercard Agent Pay and Visa Intelligent Commerce launch a day apart; Coinbase x402. (07)
- **Aug 1, 2025** — **Walmart's supplier traceability program goes live with chargebacks** — the real near-term FSMA forcing function. (03)
- **Aug 7, 2025** — FDA proposes 30-month FSMA 204 extension; **Nov 2025: Congress statutorily bars enforcement before July 20, 2028** (Continuing Appropriations Act). Guaranteed schema, uncertain deadline. (03)
- **Sep–Oct 2025** — OpenAI+Stripe ACP / Instant Checkout; Google AP2 (W3C VC mandate chains — the wave's best design idea); Visa TAP; Stripe/Paradigm Tempo. (07)
- **Nov 2025** — Amazon sues Perplexity over its shopping agent (injunction later overturned on appeal, narrowly). (07)
- **Jan 2026** — **UCP (Google+Shopify, Apache 2.0, 20+ retailers)** — the credible consumer-side consolidation candidate. (07)
- **Feb–Mar 2026** — OpenAI deprecates in-chat Instant Checkout (~30 merchants ever live); **Shopify flips Agentic Storefronts on by default for 5.6M stores** — zero-integration onboarding wins again; Tempo mainnet; Stripe integrates x402. (07)
- **Jun–Jul 2026** — GrubMarket ships a distributor Sales AI Agent (closest proprietary threat in food, IPO-track, $2.4B revenue); Visa, Mastercard, Ripple join x402 (75M payments moving only ~$24M/30d — rails without an economy). (07)
- **Aug 2026** — *Snapshot date of this research.* ONDC retail down ~30% from its Oct 2024 peak after incentive cuts, 85% of sellers inactive; Namma Yatri at ~130M rides on ~$5M spend remains the one unambiguous open-network success. **No open protocol exists for negotiated, escrow-settled, agent-native B2B physical-goods trade. The whitespace DTP targets is empty; the race is against proprietary platforms, not rival protocols.** (06, 07)
